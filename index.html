FIBER
Route the light. Dodge the bombs.

Support

Fiber is a puzzle game about routing light from a source to a target with mirrors, splitters, portals and more, past explosives, through dark chambers and around a nano bot. Something not working, or a question about a level? Write to tulparapps@gmail.com and say which device you play on and the version shown under Settings > About.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Everything Fiber needs is inside the app: the levels, the music and the sound, and your progress, which is saved on your device. It plays the same with no connection at all, in airplane mode or underground.

Where is my progress kept?

On your device, inside the game: in the app's own storage on iPhone and iPad, in the game's save file on a computer, and in the browser's local storage in the web version. Nothing is kept online, so progress doesn't move between devices, and deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes it.

How do I start over?

Settings > Progress > Reset progress erases your ratings and starts again from Level 01. It can't be undone.

A level seems impossible.

Every level is checked by a solver when the game is built: each has a solution with the pieces it lists. The level's hint shows when it opens, and the level card lists the pieces it needs. Proximity mines heat up when light passes next to them, not only through them; locked pieces (a small padlock) can't be moved or turned.

The sound is off on my iPhone.

Fiber's sound follows the silent switch, like most games. In Settings, Sound turns everything on or off, and Music turns off just the music, so the effects play on.

Colours are hard to tell apart.

Settings > Colour-blind mode uses clearer red, green and blue and marks each colour with a shape: ▲ red, ● green, ■ blue. Reduce motion turns off screen shake and most sparks.
