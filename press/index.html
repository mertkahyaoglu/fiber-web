About the game

Every level is a chamber with a source and a target. Light travels in straight lines; mirrors bend it, splitters send it two ways, couplers add weak beams back up, patch cords jump it over walls and one-way portals carry it through them. Then the chamber starts fighting back: explosives that heat up under the beam, proximity mines that only need it to pass close by, keep-alive bombs that go off when the light leaves them, walls and bombs that patrol their tracks, chambers with live edges that go off if the light touches them, dark chambers where only the light shows the way, and colour: prisms, filters and stained glass. At the end waits a nano bot that has to be burnt down in its own colour.

Wins are rated in Wi-Fi bars, from the time taken and the explosives touched. Bars earn lux, the game's currency, and lux buys new pieces in the in-game store for the chapters ahead. Winning with fewer pieces than a level lists pays a bonus.

Features

  • 53 hand-made levels in 16 chapters, each one checked by a solver, then endless generated levels
  • Eight kinds of piece and a new hazard in almost every chapter, introduced one at a time
  • A synthwave soundtrack generated as you play
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking; plays offline
  • Colour-blind mode (every colour also gets a shape) and a reduce-motion option
  • Game Center achievements
