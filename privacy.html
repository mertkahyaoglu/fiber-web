Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Fiber collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't connect to the internet to play.

What the game stores

To remember your progress and settings, Fiber keeps them on your device: your best rating on each level, and your settings (sound, music, motion, colour-blind mode, vibration). On iPhone and iPad this lives in the app's own storage; on a computer, in the game's save file; in a web browser, in that browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and nobody else can see it. Deleting the app (or Settings > Progress > Reset progress) deletes it.

Steam

If you play Fiber through Steam, Steam may copy the game's save file to your Steam account so that your progress follows you between computers (Steam Cloud). That copy is handled by Valve under the Steam privacy policy; Fiber itself sends nothing.

Children

Fiber is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A version of Fiber that collected data would say so here before it shipped.
